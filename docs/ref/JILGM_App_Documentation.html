1 System Overview

The JILGM-STBC Attendance System is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that enables fast, digital attendance tracking for Sunday services. It runs on any phone or computer with a browser.

How It Works (End-to-End)

Printed QR Poster➔ Member Scans & Registers➔ Personal QR Emailed➔ Usher Scans on Sunday➔ Report Auto-Generated

System Components

ComponentPurposeWhere
Attendance App (PWA)Check-in interface used by ushersGitHub Pages (online)
Google FormMember registration formGoogle Forms
Google SheetsDatabase (members, attendance, reports)Church Google account
Google Apps ScriptBackend automation (QR emails, sync API)Church Google account
💡 Zero cost. All components use free Google services and free GitHub hosting. No subscriptions or servers needed.

2 Initial Setup (One-Time)

This setup only needs to be done once. After this, the system runs automatically.

A. Install the App on Usher Phones

1
Open the app URL in ChromeOpen Chrome on your phone and navigate to the app URL provided by the admin.
2
Install as appTap the three-dot menu (top right) and select "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen." The app will appear on your home screen with the JILGM logo.
3
Configure the sync URLOpen the app, go to Settings tab, paste the Google Apps Script URL in the "Script URL" field, and tap "Test Connection." You should see "Connected."
4
Sync membersTap "Sync Now" to pull all registered members from the database. You should see "Synced! X members loaded."
✅ The Script URL only needs to be entered once per device. It is saved locally and persists across app restarts.

B. Print the Registration QR Code

1
Generate QR imageOpen this URL in a browser to get the QR code image of your registration form. Save the image.
2
Print on poster/tarpaulinPrint the QR code large enough to scan from a distance. Place it at the church entrance, lobby, or registration area.
3
Add instructionsBelow the QR, add text like: "Scan this QR code to register. You will receive your personal attendance QR via email."

3 Member Registration Flow

Members register themselves by scanning the printed QR poster. No usher action required.

What Members Do

1
Scan the QR posterUsing their phone camera or any QR scanner app.
2
Fill out the Google FormName, email, phone, birthday, outreach location, ministry, gender, civil status, etc.
3
SubmitThe system automatically registers them and assigns a unique Member ID (e.g., MEM-001).
4
Receive personal QR via emailWithin seconds, the member receives an email with their personal QR code image. This is what they show to ushers on Sunday.

What Happens Behind the Scenes

ActionAutomated By
Form response saved to Members sheetGoogle Apps Script (onFormSubmit trigger)
Unique Member ID generated (MEM-XXX)Google Apps Script
QR code image generatedQR Code API
QR saved to Google DriveGoogle Apps Script
Email sent with QR attachmentGoogle Apps Script (GmailApp)
Member appears on usher app (within 60s)Auto-sync
⚠ Email required. Members must provide a valid email address to receive their QR code. If they don't have email, an usher can manually add them via the app and show them their QR from the Member Detail screen.

4 Check-In Modes

The app offers 4 ways to check in members. Use whichever is fastest for your situation.

Mode 1: QR Scan

The primary check-in method. Members show their personal QR code (from email or saved screenshot), and the usher scans it with the app.

FeatureDetail
CameraFront and back camera supported (tap "Flip Camera" to switch)
SpeedScans in under 1 second
FeedbackGreen overlay with "Welcome, [Name]!" + audio beep
DuplicatesIf already checked in, shows warning "already checked in"
Unknown QRIf QR doesn't match any member, shows "Member not found"

Mode 2: Quick Tap

A visual grid of all member avatars. Tap once to check in.

FeatureDetail
DisplayCircular avatars with initials (or photo if uploaded)
Visual cuesGreen ring = already checked in. Orange "N" badge = new/returning member.
FiltersFilter by ministry. Toggle "All" vs "Not Here" (show only unchecked).
Best forSmall-to-medium congregations where ushers know members by face

Mode 3: Search

Type a member's name to find and check them in.

FeatureDetail
SearchLive search as you type (shows first 10 matches)
Already hereShows "Already here" label next to checked-in members
Best forWhen member forgot their QR and you need to find them by name

Mode 4: Visitor (Walk-in)

For first-time guests who haven't registered yet.

FeatureDetail
AccessTap "New Visitor" card in Search mode
FieldsName (required), Phone (optional), Notes (optional)
ResultVisitor is added as a member AND checked in simultaneously
Follow-upThey can complete full registration via the form later
💡 Recommendation: Use QR Scan as primary mode for speed. Use Quick Tap for members who forgot their QR. Use Search as a fallback. Use Visitor for walk-ins.

Member Detail Panel

Tap any member in the list to open their full profile. Shows:

  • Avatar (photo or colored initials)
  • Contact info (phone, address, email)
  • Birthday and computed age
  • Age group classification
  • Last 10 Sundays attendance trail (green dots = present, gray = absent)

  • Action buttons: Edit, Show QR, Resend QR, Delete

Membership Status Pipeline

First Time Guest➔ Returning Guest➔ Regular Attendee

Transitions are automatic: after 4 check-ins, a member graduates to Regular Attendee. If a Regular becomes inactive and returns, they become Returning Guest until they attend 4 more times.

8 Outreach Tracking

The system supports tracking members from multiple outreach locations with different attendance rules.

Supported Locations

LocationService ScheduleInactivity Rule
Sto. Tomas (Main)Every SundayInactive after 8 consecutive Sundays absent
Tanauan1st Sunday of monthInactive after 8 consecutive 1st Sundays absent (~8 months)
NaujanVariesNo auto-inactive (grace for remote area)
SocorroVariesNo auto-inactive (grace for remote area)

How Outreach Affects Reports

  • Sto. Tomas members are counted in the age bracket breakdown (Youth, YPro, Mothers, Fathers)
  • Other outreach members (Tanauan, Naujan, Socorro) appear as separate line items in the report, NOT counted in age brackets
  • "New" count only includes First Time Guests from Sto. Tomas. First-timers from other outreaches show under their outreach line.

9 Sync & Offline Usage

Auto-Sync (Background)

Every 60 seconds, the app silently:

  • Pushes any pending offline check-ins to Google Sheets
  • Pulls today's attendance (from other usher devices)
  • Pulls new members (registered via form)

No toast or popup appears. This is completely silent.

Manual Sync

Tap "Sync Now" in Settings to force an immediate sync. Shows a toast with results.

Offline Mode

If internet is unavailable (e.g., poor signal at church):

  • All check-in modes still work normally
  • Check-ins are saved locally in IndexedDB
  • A "Pending Changes" counter shows how many are queued
  • When internet returns, auto-sync uploads everything
✅ Multi-device: Multiple ushers can check in simultaneously on different phones. All data merges correctly via the backend. No conflicts or duplicates.

PWA Installation

Once installed as a PWA ("Add to Home Screen"), the app:

  • Opens fullscreen (no browser bar)
  • Has its own home screen icon with JILGM logo
  • Works offline (cached by service worker)
  • Appears in app switcher like a native app

10 Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
"No sync URL configured"Go to Settings, paste the Script URL, and tap Save.
Sync shows 0 membersThe Script URL may be outdated. Ask admin to check if the Web App was redeployed as a new version.
Camera not working (QR Scan)The app needs HTTPS to access the camera. Make sure you're using the online URL (not a local file). Also check browser camera permissions.
Member not found on scanThe member may not be synced yet. Tap Sync Now, then try again. Or use Quick Tap / Search mode.
App opens in browser (not standalone)Uninstall the shortcut, revisit the URL, and re-install. May need to open twice for the service worker to activate.
Toast notification sticks on screenPull down to refresh the page. This is a rare caching issue that resolves after the next app update.
Report email not receivedCheck the Report Email field in Settings. Also check spam folder. Ensure the Script URL is connected.
Member registered but not appearingWait 60 seconds for auto-sync, or tap Sync Now manually.
QR email not received by memberCheck if the email field was filled correctly in the form. Open the member's detail in the app and tap "Resend QR."
Internet lost during serviceNo problem. Continue checking in normally. All data is saved locally and syncs when internet returns.
⚠ If all else fails: Clear the app's site data in Chrome settings, reopen the URL, re-enter the Script URL, and Sync Now. This resets the local cache while preserving all server-side data.
