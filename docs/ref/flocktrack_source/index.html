What is FlockTrack?

FlockTrack is a phone app for JILGM Sto. Tomas Batangas that tracks Sunday attendance. It works on any phone or tablet, can be installed like a regular app, and works even without internet. All data goes to Google Sheets.

Launch Day Plan

What happens on the first Sunday we use FlockTrack.

1. Before Sunday

Print QR code posters that link to the registration form. Place at entrance, chairs, or project on screen. Have 2-3 ushers with approved devices ready.

2. Members Arrive

New members: Scan the QR poster with their phone. It opens a form where they register themselves.
Known members: Ushers check them in using Quick Tap or Search mode.

3. After Service

New registrations are automatically processed. If someone accidentally registers twice, the system catches it and flags it for review.

4. During the Week

Upon registration, the member's personal QR code is automatically sent to the email address they provided in the form.

5. Next Sundays

Members show their personal QR on their phone screen. Usher scans it with FlockTrack = instant check-in. Fast and easy.

Tip: For launch day, Quick Tap and Search modes are the fastest way to check in known members. The QR poster handles new member registration without needing usher help.

Member Registration

Two ways members get into the system.

Self-Registration (QR Poster)

  • Member scans QR poster at church
  • Google Form opens on their phone
  • They fill in Name, Phone, Birthday, etc.
  • Submit. Done. No usher needed.
  • System checks for duplicates automatically

Manual Add (by Usher)

  • Usher opens Members tab in FlockTrack
  • Taps the + button
  • Fills in member details
  • Good for elderly or those without smartphones
  • Also checks for duplicates

QR Codes Explained

There are TWO different QR codes. Don't confuse them.

📌 Poster QR (Registration)

  • ONE QR code, same for everyone
  • Printed on posters at church entrance
  • Links to the Google Form
  • For NEW members to register themselves
  • Used only once per person

👤 Personal QR (Check-In)

  • UNIQUE per member
  • Generated after registration
  • Automatically sent to member's email upon registration
  • Member saves it on their phone
  • Shows it every Sunday for check-in
  • Usher scans it = instant attendance

FAQ

Can it work without internet?

Yes. Check-ins save locally and sync when you reconnect.

What's the difference between the two QR codes?

Poster QR = for registration (one QR, everyone scans it to sign up).
Personal QR = for check-in (unique per member, scanned every Sunday).

How do members get their personal QR?

After they register, their personal QR code is automatically sent to the email address they provided. They save it on their phone and show it each Sunday.

What if someone registers twice?

The system catches it. Duplicate entries go to a "Review" sheet instead. Admin checks and decides.

What if QR scanner doesn't work?

Allow camera permission. Or just use Quick Tap or Search mode instead.

How many ushers can use it?

Unlimited. Multiple ushers can check in members at the same time from different devices.

Is there a backup?

Yes. The system automatically backs up all data to a separate spreadsheet on the 1st of every month. No action needed.
